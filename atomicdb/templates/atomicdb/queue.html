The queue, by lane

The fleet is shared out between lanes. An account that ran a worker in the last {{ window_days }} days gets a lane of its own; everybody else, signed in or not, shares one lane called Commons. Alt accounts buy nothing, because they all land in the same commons. Inside a lane, requests are ordered by the nodes that account already has queued, so a big batch interleaves instead of walling the queue.
