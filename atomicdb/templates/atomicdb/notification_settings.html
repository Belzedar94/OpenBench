What my workers take first

Your workers always take people's requests before the server's own exploration work. This sets the order among the requests.

Mine first

Queue order
The shared queue as everyone sees it. Yours wait their turn and the share list is not used.
Others first

"Your requests" also covers positions you asked for that someone else had requested first, and the same goes for the accounts you share with.

Their requests count like yours with Mine first and go before everyone with Others first. Write anonymous for requests made without an account.
